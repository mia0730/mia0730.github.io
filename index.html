The three formulas

Drill these until they're automatic. Full practice on the Zoning Math page.

FAR = Total gross building area ÷ Lot area
OSR = Open space ÷ Lot area × 100   where Open space = Lot − Footprint − Parking
Lot Coverage = (Footprint + Parking) ÷ Lot area × 100   = 100% − OSR
The trap

FAR uses all floors added together (4 floors × 10,000 sf = 40,000 sf). Lot coverage and OSR use the footprint only — one floor — plus the parking lot. Mixing those up is the single easiest way to lose points on this exam.

How to use the 50 minutes

  • Don't stall on one question. It's Brightspace and it closes automatically. Answer everything you know first, flag the rest.
  • Do the calculation question early, while you're fresh and while there's plenty of time on the clock. It's the one question that takes real minutes.
  • Matching is free points if you did the flashcards. Do it fast and bank the time.
  • On true/false, watch for absolutes. "Sheeting is always required in rock" is false — rock generally doesn't need sheeting at all.
  • Get to class on time. One attempt, no second chances.
